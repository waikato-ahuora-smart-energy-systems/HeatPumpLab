# Nine additional binary-fluid screens

Start nine new, independent binary families from zero observations. Each receives 800 global BO candidate evaluations, failures included, for 7,200 total. Four searches run concurrently with one numerical thread per process; completed family slots move to the next queued pair. Existing completed propane-family data remain unchanged and are used only as comparison references.

Pairs, in queue order: Propane/Cyclopentane, Butane/Pentane, Isobutane/Pentane, Butane/Isopentane, Isobutane/Isopentane, Butane/Cyclopentane, Isobutane/Cyclopentane, Butane/Isobutane, Pentane/Isopentane. Butane and Pentane denote the normal isomers. Composition x[3] is always the mass fraction of the first named component, and x[4] its complement.

Thermodynamics use an exact copy of the frozen v17 model, including internal PH fallback and VHC_total = Q_H_total/(V_suc_1+V_suc_2). The 200°C sink, 40°C source, 10°C evaporator inlet, 20 K gas-cooler minimum approach, existing compressor efficiency model and inner pressure policy are unchanged. Optimised variables are RHX1 and RHX2 cold-side lifts (0–100 K each, combined limit 123.333333 K), compressor-2 power share (0.1–0.9), and first-component mass fraction (0–1).

Global BoTorch qLogEHVI maximises COP and VHC_total with reference (0,0), 64 MC samples and q=1. The initial 32 designs cover composition endpoints, zero-lift designs and Sobol points. All successful observations train the global GP during this bounded screen. Every eighth adaptive proposal explores by maximin; GP hyperparameters refit every five evaluations. Candidate scoring batches are eight, continuous optimisation has six restarts, raw_samples 128, maxiter 60, restart batch one and initialisation batch eight. Seed 20260912. The 128-point subset method is reserved for subsequent local refinement of promising fronts after this global screen.

Each native evaluation has a 180-second limit, no timeout retry and a fresh worker. Infrastructure failures stop the affected family without recording false thermodynamic failures; other queued families continue. Checkpoints retain observations and GP parameters. Each complete observed COP–VHC_total frontier is independently replayed, comparing COP, VHC, SEC, pressure, maximum reported refrigerant temperature and compressor pressure ratios at rtol 1e-6 and atol 1e-9. Rejected points are excluded and newly exposed front points replayed. Replays and one integration smoke probe per family are additional evaluations outside the 800-candidate search budgets.

The three cyclopentane pairs return native SETUP warning -117: binary interaction parameters are unavailable, so alternative mixing rules from a similar substance are used. These predictions are exploratory property estimates. The six other pairs initialise without warnings. Exact diagnostics are preserved in preflight.json and checkpoint metadata. Repeatable numerical results do not validate an estimated mixture model. See https://pages.nist.gov/REFPROP-docs/#mixture-error-messages .

Report all nine fronts against the existing four-family reference front, failure rates, highest COP, VHC, temperature, pressure and compressor pressure ratios. Maximum reported refrigerant temperature is the maximum of compressor outlets, final discharge, gas-cooler outlet and evaporator outlet; it is not a wall temperature or a chemical-stability limit. No unprovided equipment or decomposition-temperature limits are imposed.

Generate a provisional top-three local-refinement shortlist ranked by incremental hypervolume beyond the reference front, with own-family hypervolume breaking ties. The previous reference families had 4,000 evaluations each; this is not an equal-budget comparison. Numerical ranking does not resolve chemical stability or estimated-mixture-model uncertainty. No automatic new 4,000-evaluation runs are scheduled.
